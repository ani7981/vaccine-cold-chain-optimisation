Feature Engineering Pipeline

Leakage-Free Thermodynamic Feature Matrix

pipeline
1

Dual-Probe Redundancy Aggregation

Dual thermal_shipper_temp readings (first/last) averaged into consensus temp_mean; discrepancy between probes becomes a drift detection feature.

2

Kinetic Rate-of-Change Features

temp_delta_1h, temp_delta_3h, temp_accel — first and second-order derivatives encoding thermal momentum. No absolute time counters to prevent leakage.

3

Ambient Thermal Gradient

thermal_gradient = room_temp − temp_mean. Captures convective heat ingress pressure. High gradient under compressor failure causes rapid SHAP excursion escalation.

4

4-Hour Forward Lookahead Target (Zero Leakage)

risk_next_4h: binary flag — was there a failure (discarded / OOB hours ≥5 / expiry ≤0) within the next 4 observations? Computed per-batch with reverse rolling max to prevent future-data leakage.

5

Rolling Statistical Moments

temp_rolling_mean_3h and temp_rolling_std_3h over 3-observation windows. Captures sustained excursion vs. transient spike; both top SHAP global drivers.
